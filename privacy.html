Privacy Policy – CB Brand Worker

Last updated: 7 October 2026

CB Brand Worker ("the app") is an internal tool operated by Christopher Broomhead (Chris Broomhead Coaching). It is used only with Google accounts owned or administered by the operator.

Data the app accesses

When an account owner grants permission, the app may access Gmail messages and metadata, Google Calendar events, and basic profile information (email address) for that account.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to give the account owner inbox monitoring, triage, alerts, draft replies and calendar management for their own business. Data is processed on the operator's own computer. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties except where required by law.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's machine. Message content is kept only as long as needed for triage and alerting. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
